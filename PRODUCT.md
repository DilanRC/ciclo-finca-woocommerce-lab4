# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The storefront is organized for cyclists in Costa Rica who want to browse bicycles, riding accessories, hydration, and apparel, then place an order in colones. Specific customer segments have not been defined.

## Product Purpose

Ciclo Finca presents a cycling catalog with product pages, stock visibility, a cart, and guest checkout. Success means visitors can find a category, understand the listed price and availability, and complete the order flow without confusing payment or delivery details.

## Positioning

The catalog brings mountain, gravel, and ride essentials together under a Costa Rica cycling identity. No unique supplier, service, or product claim has been confirmed.

## Operating Context

The storefront runs on WordPress and WooCommerce in Docker. Prices use CRC. Orders use a manual payment method and remain pending until the payment status is changed. The configured delivery option is local pickup.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Simple products include a name, description, image, price, and managed stock.
- Visitors can browse categories and product pages, use the cart, and check out as guests.
- This checkout does not process card payments or collect bank details. Orders are stored in the local WordPress database.
- The current catalog prices and stock counts are configured data; supplier, fulfillment address, support contacts, and product specifications beyond listed copy are open decisions and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ciclo Finca.
- Costa Rica is the geographic context and CRC is the store currency.
- Existing bicycle-and-leaf brand mark and cycling imagery are retained.
- The user chose a sporty, energetic character for this surface.
- Customer-facing copy is in natural Spanish and avoids references to coursework or academic evaluation.

## Product Principles

1. Make product category, price, and availability easy to scan.
2. Keep the order flow clear and predictable.
3. State payment and pickup details plainly.
4. Do not invent technical, supplier, delivery, or customer proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Navigation and actions must have visible text labels, keyboard focus, and touch-friendly targets. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
